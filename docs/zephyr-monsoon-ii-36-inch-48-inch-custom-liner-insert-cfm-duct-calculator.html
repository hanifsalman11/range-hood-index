Frequently Asked Questions (Zephyr Monsoon II (36-Inch & 48-Inch) Custom Liner Insert)

How many CFM do I need for Zephyr Monsoon II (36-Inch & 48-Inch) Custom Liner Insert (92,000 BTU)?

Using the HVI/NKBA professional gas formula, divide your total burner output (92,000 BTU) by 100 to get 920 base CFM. Adding a 20% static pressure buffer for elbows and the exterior roof/wall cap gives 1,100 recommended blower CFM.

Why does a 1,100 CFM range hood require an 10-inch duct and Make-Up Air?

Pushing 1,100 CFM through an undersized 6-inch pipe forces air velocity above 2,800 FPM—creating deafening turbulence and choking blower output. An 10-inch rigid duct keeps velocity at 2,018 FPM. Furthermore, IRC code M1503.4 mandates a Make-Up Air damper for any hood exceeding 400 CFM.

How much CFM do 90-degree elbows subtract from my range hood?

Every sharp 90-degree elbow adds 25 Equivalent Feet of resistance (two 45-degree elbows add only 15 Equivalent Feet), and an exterior roof/wall cap adds 30 Equivalent Feet. Keep total Equivalent Duct Length under 100 feet.
